# ZHAW University Portfolio

Selected projects from my Master of Science in Engineering (Computer Science) at ZHAW School of Engineering, undertaken as part of a double-degree programme with Victoria University of Wellington.

## Projects

- **[Cloud-Native Portfolio Decision Support](cloud_native_portfolio_system/)** – Research and experimental evaluation of cloud-native architectures for an agentic AI portfolio decision-support system, comparing deployment approaches, scalability, and systems performance.

- **[Lock Contention and Synchronisation](systems_performance/)** – Experimental comparison of spinlocks, standard mutexes, and adaptive mutexes, investigating how lock-waiting policies affect acquisition latency under varying contention levels.
